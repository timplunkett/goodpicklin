Why we play

Open to all levels, Good Picklin’ is focused on why we all fell in love with pickleball... it’s fun. Come alone or with friends and spend your Saturday doing what Saturdays were made for: good picklin’!

What to expect

  • A managed paddle line. It’s always clear who’s up next.
  • Games by skill level. Your hosts organize matchups so games stay fun and competitive.
  • Rotating partners and opponents. Play with (and against) lots of new faces.
  • Reasonable breaks. Time between games to rehydrate and cheer on your fellow picklers.
  • Beginner and Challenge courts. When turnout calls for it, there’s a court for you, whether you’re working on your first dink or chasing your best rally.

Your hosts

Good Picklin’ is facilitated by Fishtown’s finest, Tim and Megh Plunkett.
